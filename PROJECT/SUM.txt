1. SUM Formula (Sab se behter aur aasan tarika)
Agar aap kisi range ko plus karna chahte hain, toh SUM formula istemal karein:

Excel
=SUM(A1:A5)
2. Basic Plus (+) Operator
Aap alag-alag cells ko plus nisthan (+) ke zariye bhi jod sakte hain:

Excel
=A1+A2+A3+A4+A5




Method 2: Excel 2016 / 2013 ya Purana Version (Array Formula)
Agar aapka Excel purana hai aur Method 1 se sirf ek value aa rahi hai ya error mil raha hai:

Pehle mouse se C1 se C7 tak poori range ko ikhtay select karein.

Formula bar mein yeh type karein:

Excel
=A1:A7+B1:B7
Normal Enter ki bajaye Ctrl + Shift + Enter ek saath dabayein.
