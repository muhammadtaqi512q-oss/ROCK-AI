1. SUM Formula (Sab se behter aur aasan tarika)
Agar aap kisi range ko plus karna chahte hain, toh SUM formula istemal karein:

Excel
=SUM(A1:A5)
2. Basic Plus (+) Operator
Aap alag-alag cells ko plus nisthan (+) ke zariye bhi jod sakte hain:

Excel
=A1+A2+A3+A4+A5




Tarika 2: Single Array Formula (Office 365 / Excel 2021+)
Agar aap naya Excel istemal kar rahe hain, toh sirf Cell C1 mein yeh ek formula likhein aur Enter daba dein:

Excel
=A1:A100+B1:B100
Yeh ek hi formula poori C1 se C100 tak ki range ko khud-ba-khud fill kar dega.
