A1 se A100 tak ki saari values ko aapas mein multiply karne ke liye sab se aasan tarika PRODUCT formula ka hai:

1. Range Multiply Formula (Best Tarika)
A1 se A100 tak ke tamaam numbers ko ek sath multiply karne ke liye:

Excel
=PRODUCT(A1:A100)
(Yeh formula A1 * A2 * A3 * ... * A100 ka result ek saath de dega.)



Tarika 2: Dynamic Array Formula (Office 365 / Excel 2021+)
Agar aap Office 365 ya Excel 2021+ istemal kar rahe hain, toh sirf Cell C1 mein yeh single formula likhein aur Enter daba dein:

Excel
=A1:A100*B1:B100
Yeh ek hi formula poori C1 se C100 ki range ko khud-ba-khud fill kar ke sabhi rows ko multiply kar dega.
