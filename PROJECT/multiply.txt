A1 se A100 tak ki saari values ko aapas mein multiply karne ke liye sab se aasan tarika PRODUCT formula ka hai:

1. Range Multiply Formula (Best Tarika)
A1 se A100 tak ke tamaam numbers ko ek sath multiply karne ke liye:

Excel
=PRODUCT(A1:A100)
(Yeh formula A1 * A2 * A3 * ... * A100 ka result ek saath de dega.)
