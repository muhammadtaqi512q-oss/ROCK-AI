A1 se A100 tak ki saari values ko aapas mein multiply karne ke liye sab se aasan tarika PRODUCT formula ka hai:

1. Range Multiply Formula (Best Tarika)
A1 se A100 tak ke tamaam numbers ko ek sath multiply karne ke liye:

Excel
=PRODUCT(A1:A100)
(Yeh formula A1 * A2 * A3 * ... * A100 ka result ek saath de dega.)



Method 2: Excel 2016 / 2013 ya Purana Version (Array Formula)
Agar aapka Excel purana hai aur Method 1 se sirf ek value aa rahi hai ya error mil raha hai:

Pehle mouse se C1 se C7 tak poori range ko ikhtay select karein.

Formula bar mein yeh type karein:

Excel
=A1:A7*B1:B7
Normal Enter ki bajaye Ctrl + Shift + Enter ek saath dabayein.
